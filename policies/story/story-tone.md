# Story-Tone Guidance

Policy id: `story/story-tone`
Status: draft — sign-off pending (see `MANIFEST.md`).

Purpose: steer concept and page-text stages toward the emotional goals the product commits to (spec §1 "small publishing studio that happens to know your child"; emotionalGoal from F-007 concept). Guidance is **declared as rules the stage must satisfy** — evaluated by F-008 story QA (contradiction, arc, facts), not by prompt persuasion.

## Arcs

- Every story maps to one `emotionalGoal` from the concept (confidence, bedtime calmness, adventure, kindness, curiosity…).
- The outline's `arc` must end at that goal; story QA checks the beat order ends at the goal state (F-008 §11).

## Tone rules

- Warm, gentle, and particular to the child's facts — never generic filler; every page should be personally meaningful (mission §33 filter).
- Keep events plausible inside the story's own premise; avoid scare-set dressing for the age band.
- Conflict resolves on-page; no cliff-hanger endings for the launch catalogue.
- No moralising aside; the arc carries the message.
- Facts are immutable (F-006/F-008): a tone decision never rewrites the parent's facts.

## Typography and format

- Tone decisions influence words only. Page geometry, line breaks, fonts and safe areas are deterministic rendering (GENERATION_ARCHITECTURE §11), never model output.

## Non-content

No child data, no secrets, no copied prompt material.
