## Research Standard

Do not rely only on homepages.

Inspect the actual creation journey where possible.

For every claim classify it as:

- Observed
- Inferred
- Not verifiable without purchase

Important areas:

- onboarding;
- photo upload;
- personalisation;
- generation;
- preview;
- correction;
- checkout;
- pricing;
- printing;
- shipping;
- order tracking;
- privacy;
- customer complaints.

The goal is not feature-count competition.

The goal is to identify:

> what must work extremely well for customers to trust and love this product.
