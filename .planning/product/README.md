# Product Planning

The intended product experience is:

> A small publishing studio that happens to know your child.

The application should hide most AI complexity.

The parent should primarily:

1. choose what they want to make;
2. tell us about the child;
3. select a story idea;
4. receive a polished book;
5. correct anything they dislike;
6. approve the exact book;
7. purchase it;
8. track it;
9. retain it in their family story library.

---

## Product Priorities

### P0

Category parity and dependable creation.

### P1

Reasons For Little One is meaningfully better.

### P2

Retention and delight.

### P3

Experiments.

---

## Main Differentiation Candidates

- strong character consistency;
- persistent child/family profiles;
- effortless repair;
- global character corrections;
- deeper story personalisation;
- multi-person stories;
- localisation;
- privacy;
- generation reliability;
- approval-before-print;
- excellent mobile experience.
